About the workshop

Independent multi-brand service centre in Sofia, operating since 2004.

What we do

Pistonworks is a six-bay workshop handling diagnostics, engine and transmission work, suspension, brakes, electrical faults and bodywork. Every job is written up as a repair order, split into individual service tasks, and priced from the mechanic's hourly rate.

How parts are handled

Our warehouse is managed as a separate system. When a mechanic adds parts to a job the stock is reserved rather than sold, so the same alternator cannot be promised to two cars at once. Reservations are only converted into a sale when the job is completed, and are returned to stock automatically if the job is cancelled.
